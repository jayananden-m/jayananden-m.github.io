## Introduction

Nginx. Even the name sounds like something from the early days of the internet. For those who haven't been around that long, "Nginx" is pronounced "Engine X". It’s an open-source software that has quietly become one of the most critical pieces of infrastructure on the web. You might have heard of it as a web server, maybe as a reverse proxy, or perhaps you just know it as that mysterious thing running behind the scenes on most websites. But the real magic of Nginx isn't just what it does; it's _how_ it does it. In this post, we’re going to explore the architecture of Nginx, its history, and the features that make it one of the most beloved tools for developers and system administrators around the world.
